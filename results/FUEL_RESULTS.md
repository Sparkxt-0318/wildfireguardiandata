## Improvement loop (`ROUNDS_fuel.md`)
12 rounds on dev areas. Kept: round 1 (switch off R1.9a: grass class under NLCD+WorldCover shrub; 17 → 7 false alarms), 2 (R1.1 max_S -3 → -2), 3 (R1.7 natural-vegetation share 70 → 50), 4 (R1.9 lifeform share 60 → 50), 5 (R1.1 max_S → -1), 10 (R1.9 tree-cover limit 5 → 10). Reverted: 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12. Dev F1 went from 10.59 to 11.19 and F2 from 0.98 % to 0.40 %; the F1 changes are inside the bootstrap noise (intervals overlap almost completely), and the clear gain is fewer false alarms. Sealed F1 (7.9) is lower than dev (11.2): part of the dev value is tuning to the dev plots and part is that the sealed areas differ (their consistent cells disagree more often, 11.1 % vs 7.6 %). Most of the information in the verdicts comes from rules R1.1 and R1.7 and R1.9b, which were in version 1.

## Stretch on the 29 fires (labelled stretch; `results/fuel/stretch_summary.json`)
Set: 29 rebuilt fires, 2017-2019, 13 states (not the original 29: see `firewitness/fuel/fires29/NOTES.md`, D-532). Agent run on the LF2016 map (events of the fire's own year or later ignored: amendment A2), 309,134 contradicted cells with a suggested class replaced in 28 fires (one fire had none). A minimum-travel-time spread calculation (pyretechnics head-fire rates, mean observed wind, 90 m grid) was run on the original and replaced maps and compared with arrival times built from daily perimeters, after one scale factor per fire fitted on the original map.
* Mean absolute error of arrival, replaced minus original: **-0.42 h, 95 % interval [-1.49, +0.47] (n = 28 fires)**; closer in 16 fires, further in 11, one tie. Interval includes 0.
* Spearman rank correlation, replaced minus original: **-0.0085 [-0.0180, -0.0020]**; better in 7 fires, worse in 20.
* Median absolute error of the original runs is 64 h per fire (long fires, coarse spread model), so a change of half an hour is negligible.
**Conclusion: no evidence that replacing contradicted cells improves this simple spread calculation; the rank correlation is marginally worse.** This stretch cannot test the claim fairly: the observed arrival times are interpolated from daily perimeters, the spread model is a simple surface-fire model without crown fire, spotting or time-varying wind, and the replaced classes come from neighbourhood votes. Rule R3b (observed spread faster than the class can produce) fires on many cells (for example 60,122 cells in 2017_CA_detwiler) and mostly reflects the limits of surface-fire equations and daily arrival times, not fuel errors; it carries no suggestion and does not enter the stretch replacement.

## What these numbers do not show
* **Disagreement is not error.** Plots are compared through a structural proxy (protocol section 4), not an official fuel-model call. A contradicted cell where the plot "agrees" may still be a wrong fuel model, and the reverse.
* **Interior West rangeland and woodland only.** All 20 areas and all plots are BLM AIM sites in NV, ID, UT, CO, WY, OR, AZ, MT. Nothing here speaks to eastern or southern forests, urban fuels, or Alaska. AIM plots are not placed in dense timber, so the timber rules (R1.1 on timber classes, R1.2) are graded on few dense-forest plots.
* **Not independent physics.** NLCD, tree canopy cover, WorldCover, Meta canopy height and the fuel map share satellite inputs; correlated errors would make the rules fail together and would not show up here.
* **Part of F1 is regional** (placebo above), and F1 is a ratio of disagreement rates, which does not measure how many wrong cells the agent misses: 11 % of plots in "consistent" cells still disagree with the map.
* **Recall is not reported as a gate.** The agent labels 4.8 % of cells unknown and flags 6.7 % contradicted; no claim is made that the remaining cells are correct.
* **Dev tuning.** The improvement loop used the dev plots; the sealed areas are the unbiased number.
* **Staleness.** S1 is perimeter-based (fire year, whole perimeter including unburned islands); S2 is any tree-cover loss. The stale-vs-consistent contrast is significant only for the older map M16 (n_s = 150).
* **Stretch.** See above: coarse arrivals, not the original 29 fires, simple model.
* **Korea.** `config/kr.json` is an untested stub; no Korean data was used and the 58 % figure in the brief was not reproduced.
* The sealed overlays in release assets were regenerated after the freeze with a loader that only gained an optional `cutoff_year` argument (default off); their verdicts are the same code path, their manifests carry the newer code hash.
