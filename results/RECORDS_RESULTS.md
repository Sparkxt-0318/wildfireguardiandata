## Coverage (tool 5)
Of 16,766 FPA FOD fires of 1,000 acres or more, 12,331 are linked to an MTBS event, 1,191 more lie inside a WFIGS perimeter of the same year (12,491 covered, 4,275 not). Flags per state, year and agency: `results/data/coverage/coverage_flags.csv` (2,156 ok, 160 low, 369 with no perimeters). Satellite-only candidates were written as code (`coverage.satellite_only`) but not run at national scale in this version.

Addition 2026-11-20T20:40Z: satellite-only candidates, 2015 to 2023, are now run (the line above is kept as reported earlier). 21,554 grid cells (0.02 degree, at least 8 MODIS/VIIRS detections over 2 to 60 days, not repeating in other years) with no recorded fire (FPA FOD or WFIGS) within 10 km and 10 days, joined into 9,122 candidate fires (955 with 50 or more detections): `results/data/coverage/satellite_only_candidate_fires_2015_2023.csv`, summary in `satellite_only_summary.json`. They are candidates only: agricultural and prescribed burns, flares outside the 'persistent' filter and unreported wildfires are not told apart; no ground check was made.

## Limits stated once
- FIRED was not downloaded (access refused to scripted clients), so the FIRED link is scored on a restricted pool and does not meet its target.
- Planted errors are gross, in corroborated records; they say nothing about subtle in-county errors.
- Reference-clean records use the same evidence as the repair rules.
- No trained model wrote any label or patch; likelihood ratios are fitted without labels by EM with placebo pairs.

Addition 2026-11-20T21:20Z (weather checked against gridded weather, tool 1; earlier lines unchanged): gridMET (University of Idaho, 4 km daily, public annual files `tmmx_<year>.nc` daily maximum temperature and `rmin_<year>.nc` daily minimum relative humidity, 2003 to 2013, listed in `firewitness_raw/INDEX.md`) was compared with the weather fields of the 97,923 raw ICS-209 reports of 2003 to 2013 (`C_TEMP`, `C_RH`), at the report's place and date (`firewitness/records/weather.py`, `values.clean_values(..., grid=...)`). Of 63,126 reports with a temperature, 58,575 could be checked against the grid (the rest have no valid place, date or grid cell) and 53,380 humidities. The median absolute gap between the reported temperature and the daily maximum is 4.6 F; 6,239 reports (10.7%) are more than 16 F from it, which is normal (readings are not daily maxima) and is not an error by itself. The check patches only a unit slip (Celsius written as Fahrenheit, or a humidity fraction written as a percent) and flags the rest: 44 patches, `val:weather_unit_slip`, 0.075% (0.056% to 0.101%) of checked temperatures (examples: `C_TEMP` 27 becomes 81 where the grid maximum is 89.9 F); 917 temperatures flagged "unexplained by a unit slip", 1.566% (1.468% to 1.669%). Planted Celsius-as-Fahrenheit slips in reports that agree with the grid: 300 of 300 recovered (Wilson 0.987 to 1.000), 0 wrong. What it does not show: the grid is 4 km and daily, so it cannot confirm a reading, only reject one that is off by a unit; wind speed and fuel fields were not checked against any grid; 2014 and later reports carry no weather fields in these tables. File: `results/data/weather_vs_gridmet.json`.


Addition 2026-11-20T22:50Z (perimeters made from imagery for the pre-registered sample of fires with no mapped perimeter; protocol amendment of 2026-11-20T21:05Z; earlier lines unchanged): of the 677 FPA FOD fires of 1,000 acres or more in fire years 2016 to 2023 with no MTBS link and no WFIGS perimeter, a random sample of 200 (seed 1) was run through `firewitness.caught.scar.make_perimeter` (Sentinel-2, then Landsat; window 30 days from the recorded start; recorded area from FPA FOD). Outcome classes were fixed before the run. Result: a scar was found for 127 of 200 (63.5%; Wilson 56.6% to 69.9%); of these, **created and consistent** (scar area 0.2 to 5 times the recorded area) 35 of 200 (17.5%; Wilson 12.9% to 23.4%), **created and disagreeing** 92 of 200 (46.0%; Wilson 39.2% to 52.9%) (92 with a scar under 0.2 of the recorded area, 0 over 5 times); no scar although the look was clear 31 of 200 (15.5%; Wilson 11.1% to 21.2%); no clear look 36 of 200 (18.0%; Wilson 13.3% to 23.9%); rejected by the module 6 of 200 (3.0%; Wilson 1.4% to 6.4%). Median scar area over recorded area, among scars: 0.005. What it does not show: there is no reference perimeter for these fires, so "consistent" means only that area and place agree with the record, not that the shape is right; the disagreeing perimeters may reflect wrong recorded sizes or places in FPA FOD as much as scar-module limits (not separated); the sample is limited to Sentinel-2 years and to fires of 1,000 acres or more; the first look of 31 fires (seed 0, earlier line) is not part of this sample. Files: `results/data/coverage/prereg/`.
