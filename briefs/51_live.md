Goal. Two limits of current fire labels are fixed here. One: the first hours of a fire are missing, because products wait for a strong single-frame signal. Two: labels are made after the fact. Part A recovers the true start of known fires. Part B turns that into a detector that runs live.
Protocol first. protocol/LIVE_PROTOCOL.md, committed alone before any result: fire list and splits (dev, sealed test, by fire), the false-alert budget for Part B, placebo design, gates.
Part A, trace-back. For each confirmed fire (records with satellite detections or a mapped perimeter; add prompt 50's verified fires when merged), take the raw 5 to 10 minute geostationary series at that place from both satellites that see it: the 3.9 and 11 micrometre channels, and in daytime the visible channel for a smoke plume. Model each pixel's own background (the same time of day on its previous clear days, and its neighbours). Trace back from the first standard detection to the first frame where the fire is distinguishable from that background, with an interval. Compare with the operational geostationary fire product's first detection, the first polar detection and the agency's discovery time.

* Gate E1: run the same trace-back from fake anchors where no fire happened; an onset counts only if it is beyond the 99th percentile of those.
* Gate E2: the two satellites, traced independently, agree within the stated tolerance.

Part B, live detector. A sequential test per pixel that accumulates evidence over consecutive frames, so a weak but persistent signal becomes a detection below any single-frame threshold; agreement between the two satellites counts as two witnesses; cloud is masked from an independent product. Fit it on the dev fires' trace-back labels and on placebo series. Candidate settings and features are kept only if they raise the held-out replay score; nothing fitted here ever writes a label.

* Replay, strictly causal: only frames time-stamped before the decision, with realistic delivery delay, on the sealed test fires.
* Gate D1: at the frozen false-alert budget, the detector alerts before the operational product on the sealed fires, interval excluding zero. Report share of fires caught earlier, median lead in minutes, false alerts per day, by final fire size; and the whole curve, not one point.
