Goal and headline. Satellite fire labels miss the small fires. The headline of this track is one count: fires that an agency recorded, that no standard satellite product labelled, and that now have a verified label. Report it by size class.
Protocol first. protocol/CAUGHT_PROTOCOL.md, committed alone before any result: the definition of "standard label" (any active-fire detection or burned-area product in the record's space and time window; name the products), the label tiers below, the placebo design, dev and sealed test splits, gates.

Method.

1. Scar finder. For each recorded fire, within the record's uncertainty radius and date window, look for a burn scar in 10 to 20 m and 30 m imagery: present after, absent before. Mask cloud, shadow and snow. Reject harvest, ploughing and seasonal browning by comparing the same dates in other years and the neighbouring land.
2. More looks. Add every public heat sensor you can reach, not only the usual two: all VIIRS and MODIS satellites, the 30 m active-fire product, short-wave hot spots in 20 m imagery, the other 1 km thermal sensors, and at night the bands where the background is dark (sources from memory; confirm what is truly available).
3. Evidence that is not heat: the scar, smoke in daytime visible imagery, agency perimeters.
4. Label tiers through core's evidence combination. Verified: two independent kinds of evidence agree in place and time. Probable: one. Record-only: the fire is kept as a labelled fire with an honest radius and time window and the reason no pixel label is possible (cloud, no clear look, below the floor). No recorded fire is dropped.
5. Three states and a floor. Every place and time is fire, verified no fire, or unknown. Each "verified no fire" carries the smallest fire that would have been seen there and then, computed from pixel size, sensor noise, view angle and background using the sensors' published algorithm documents.
6. A scar module other tracks can call (prompt 49 uses it to make missing perimeters).

Guards and gates.

* C1, placebo: the same pipeline at matched places and dates with no recorded fire (same land cover, season, region; at least as many as the fires). At most 1% may reach the verified tier.
* C2, truth for small fires: mapped small fires and mapped prescribed burns (known place, date and area) held out as a sealed test. Recall by size class must beat the standard label's recall, interval excluding zero.
* C3: inputs hashed and never modified; no trained model writes a label; no smoothing or interpolation that puts fire where no evidence does.
